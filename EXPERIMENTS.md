# TerraWater evidence experiments

## Experiment 001 — Forest Pond and Lake Kuchnia Water Loss

**Status:** active evidence collection / quantitative validation in progress  
**AOI:** `53.591400, 19.010717`  
**Period:** `1990–2026`

Experiment files:

`experiments/experiment_001_forest_pond_kuchnia/`

Current working observation: a forest pond visible in older imagery has undergone a very large reduction and appears close to complete loss of persistent open-water signal in recent imagery.

Current working magnitude: approximately **2.5 ha / 25,000 m², near 100%**, explicitly **provisional** until corrected common-grid spectral measurement and manually verified pond geometry are complete.

Authoritative image-heavy evidence is stored in:

https://github.com/Terraforming-Planet/Polar-Sun-Moon-Analysis/tree/annual-best-53-591400-19-010717/experiments/experiment_001_pond_forest_kuchnia

### Closure gate

Experiment 001 is closed only after:

- corrected spring series;
- autumn series and explicit non-autumn handling of 2026;
- preserved error/review archive;
- multi-source image-first integrity audit;
- 1990 vs 2026 common-grid area calculation with uncertainty;
- manual pond geometry verification;
- final conclusion labelled supported / not supported / inconclusive.

### Planned program after Evidence 001

Collect approximately five verified evidence cases, then test/train TerraWater on NVIDIA L4 and subsequently survey lakes, ponds, rivers and canals within 100 km of Evidence 001 before broader scaling.
