# Experiment 001 — pond geometry correction (2026-08-14)

An image-first review before final spectral measurement found that an earlier provisional pond seed was too far east, near the western side of Lake Kuchnia.

The actual disappearing forest pond is repeatedly visible in the fixed 2 km imagery around approximately display pixel **x≈160, y≈320** in older clear scenes.

For a 2000 m / 1024 px crop, the image-derived seed is approximately:

- **690 m west** of AOI center;
- **375 m north** of AOI center;
- approximate coordinate **53.594595, 19.000140**.

Older imagery shows a distinct dark curved/L-shaped water feature at this location. Recent imagery shows the same basin as a changed/drier feature with little or no comparable persistent dark open-water footprint.

The corrected common-grid spectral workflow in the Polar evidence repo uses this location. The older provisional seed is rejected.

This remains a measurement seed, not the final basin polygon. Final area must still pass manual boundary verification against multiple years.
